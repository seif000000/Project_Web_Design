Book Title: Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone

Author: J.K. Rowling

Publication Year: 1997

Pages: 309

Description: The first book in the beloved Harry Potter series introduces readers to Harry Potter, an orphan who discovers he is a wizard on his eleventh birthday. Attending Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, he makes new friends, learns about magic, and uncovers the mystery of the Sorcerer’s Stone — all while facing the dark wizard Lord Voldemort for the first time.
