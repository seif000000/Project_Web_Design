Book Title: Little Women

Author: Louisa May Alcott

Publication Year: 1868

Pages: 759 (combined edition of both volumes)

Description: A beloved coming-of-age novel that follows the four March sisters—Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy—as they grow up during the American Civil War. Through their joys, struggles, and dreams, the story explores themes of family, love, ambition, and independence. Jo March’s spirited pursuit of writing and self-discovery has made her one of literature’s most iconic heroines.
