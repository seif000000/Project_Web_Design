Book Title: The Hobbit

Author: J.R.R. Tolkien

Publication Year: 1937

Pages: 310

Description: A classic fantasy novel that follows the journey of Bilbo Baggins, a reluctant hobbit who embarks on an unexpected adventure with a group of dwarves to reclaim their homeland from the dragon Smaug. Along the way, Bilbo encounters trolls, elves, goblins, and discovers a mysterious ring that will shape the fate of Middle-earth.
