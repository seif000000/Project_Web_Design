Book Title: Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

Author: J.K. Rowling

Publication Year: 1999

Pages: 435

Description: The third book in the Harry Potter series follows Harry’s third year at Hogwarts, where news breaks that the dangerous prisoner Sirius Black has escaped from Azkaban and is supposedly hunting Harry. As mysterious events unfold, Harry learns shocking truths about his parents, his past, and the man he thought was his enemy.
