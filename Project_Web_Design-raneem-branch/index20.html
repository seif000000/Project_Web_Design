Book Title: the Kite runner

Author: Khaled Hosseini

Publication Year: 2003

Pages: 371

Description: This powerful debut novel tells the story of Amir, a boy from Kabul, and his complicated friendship with Hassan, the son of his father’s servant. Spanning from Afghanistan’s peaceful pre-war days to its turbulent modern era, the story explores guilt, redemption, and the enduring power of friendship. The Kite Runner captures the emotional cost of betrayal and the possibility of forgiveness in a changing world.
