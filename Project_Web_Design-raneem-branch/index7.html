Book Title: Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets

Author: J.K. Rowling

Publication Year: 1998

Pages: 341

Description: The second book in the Harry Potter series follows Harry’s return to Hogwarts, where mysterious attacks turn students to stone and strange messages appear on the walls. As fear spreads through the school, Harry, Ron, and Hermione uncover the legend of the Chamber of Secrets and face the monster lurking within.
