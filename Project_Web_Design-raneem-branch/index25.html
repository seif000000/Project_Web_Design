Book Title: How to Read a Film

Author: James Monaco

Publication Year: 2009

Pages: 729

Description: This influential work presents film (and media more broadly) as an art form, a technology, a language, a history, and a theoretical field. Monaco explores how films create meaning, their relation to other narrative media such as novels, painting, television, and music, and covers major developments in technology (image, sound), film language (signs, syntax), the history and theory of film, the emergence of multimedia and digital contexts.
