Book Title: Dune

Author: Frank Herbert

Publication Year: 1965

Pages: 412

Description: A science fiction masterpiece set on the desert planet Arrakis, Dune follows Paul Atreides, heir to a noble family entrusted with the planet’s valuable spice trade. As political betrayal and rebellion erupt, Paul must embrace his destiny as the prophesied leader who will change the fate of humanity. The novel explores themes of power, religion, ecology, and survival, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest sci-fi novels ever written.
