Book Title: The Art of Captain America

Author: Alan Cowsill

Publication Year: 2025 (26 August)

Pages: Approximately 184 pages

Description: A comprehensive illustrated look at the eight-decade history of Captain America — from his creation in 1940, through his various identities (Steve Rogers, Sam Wilson) and inspired spin-offs (such as Captain Carter and Iron Patriot). This volume features exclusive interviews with artists and creators, full-color panels, cover art, behind-the-scenes illustrations, and explores how the character has evolved and remained culturally relevant over time.
