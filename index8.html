Book Title: Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

Author: Thomas Robotham

Publication Year: 2000

Pages: 734

Description: The fourth book in the Harry Potter series centers on the Triwizard Tournament, a magical competition between three wizarding schools. Harry is unexpectedly chosen as a fourth contestant and faces a series of dangerous tasks. As the challenges unfold, dark forces rise again, marking the return of Lord Voldemort and changing Harry’s world forever.
