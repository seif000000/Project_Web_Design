Book Title: The Lord of the Rings

Author: J.R.R. Tolkien

Publication Year: 1954–1955 (originally published in three volumes)

Pages: Approximately 1,216 (combined edition)

Description: An epic high-fantasy saga set in the richly imagined world of Middle-earth. The story follows Frodo Baggins, a humble hobbit, who embarks on a perilous quest to destroy the One Ring—an artifact of immense power created by the dark lord Sauron. Alongside his loyal companions, Frodo faces trials of courage, friendship, and sacrifice in a battle between good and evil that defines the fate of the world.
