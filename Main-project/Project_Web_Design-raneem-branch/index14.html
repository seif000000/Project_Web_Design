Book Title: Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix

Author: J.K. Rowling

Publication Year: 2003

Pages: 870

Description: The fifth book in the Harry Potter series sees Harry returning to Hogwarts amid growing denial of Voldemort’s return. As the Ministry of Magic interferes with the school through the cruel Dolores Umbridge, Harry forms “Dumbledore’s Army” to teach his friends defensive spells. The story explores themes of rebellion, leadership, and loss as Harry faces visions connected to Voldemort and the prophecy that binds their fates.
