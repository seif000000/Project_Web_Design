Book Title: Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince

Author: J.K. Rowling

Publication Year: 2005

Pages: 607

Description: The sixth installment in the Harry Potter series delves into Voldemort’s past and the dark secrets that shaped him. With Dumbledore’s guidance, Harry explores the mystery of the Horcruxes—objects containing pieces of Voldemort’s soul. Meanwhile, tensions rise at Hogwarts, and an old book belonging to the “Half-Blood Prince” leads Harry down unexpected paths.
